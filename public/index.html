Horizontal motion

No forces act horizontally, so ax = 0 and vx is constant.

Vertical motion

Gravity is the only vertical acceleration: ay = −g. Vertical velocity changes continuously and is zero at the apex.

Independence of motion

Horizontal and vertical motion can be analyzed separately. The two components combine to make the curved path you see.

Key idea

Launch angle splits the same initial speed into different components:

vx = v0 cos θ
vy = v0 sin θ

A steeper angle buys more hang time and height, but less horizontal speed. Complementary angles such as 30° and 60° trade those effects and can land equally far.

Launch Angle vs. Horizontal Range

R(θ) = v₀² sin(2θ) / g · Peak at 45° when heights are equal.

Launch Angle vs. Maximum Height

H(θ) = v₀² sin²(θ) / (2g) · Height keeps increasing toward 90°.
